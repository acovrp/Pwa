This is the Dream Protocol — a lateral-thinking discipline that breaks pattern-matching on ambitious problems. Core idea: the orthodox answer is the "20-second solve"; there is often a "5-second lateral solve" that violates an unstated assumption. The protocol forces you to (1) state the canonical answer, (2) excavate load-bearing assumptions, (3) imagine a team that solved it in 5 seconds, (4) violate at least one assumption to generate genuinely lateral approaches, (5) choose lateral, (6) end with a Next Assumption to Test (an experiment, not a task).
